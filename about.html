About AnongLuto?

A web-based Filipino Recipe Finder designed to help home cooks discover delicious dishes with ingredients they already have.

Problem Statement

Many Filipino home cooks struggle to decide what to cook with limited ingredients. Recipe books and websites often require specific items that may not be available in the pantry. AnongLuto? solves this by matching available ingredients to authentic Filipino recipes.

Target Users

  • Home cooks with limited ingredients
  • Students living away from home
  • Busy professionals wanting quick meals
  • Anyone craving authentic Filipino food
  • Beginners learning to cook Filipino dishes

Website Features

  • Ingredient-based recipe matching
  • Live search across all recipes
  • Category filtering (Manok, Baboy, Isda, Gulay)
  • Detailed recipe modal with instructions
  • Print-friendly recipe pages
  • Favorite recipes with localStorage
  • Responsive mobile-first design

Technology Used

Built entirely with core web technologies — no frameworks, no libraries (except Google Fonts and Font Awesome for icons).
